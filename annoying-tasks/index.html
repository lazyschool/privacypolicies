Privacy Policy

Annoying Tasks — com.annoyingtasks.app

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Annoying Tasks does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There is no server, no account system, and no analytics.

What the app accesses

The app opens only the PDFs and photos that you explicitly choose through your device’s own file and photo pickers. Those files are processed entirely on your device, and results are written back to storage you select. Nothing is uploaded.

Network access

The released version of Annoying Tasks does not hold the Android INTERNET permission. The app is technically incapable of connecting to any network. You can verify this yourself in your device’s Settings, under App info › Permissions.

Third parties

The app uses Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition and image segmentation. These models are bundled inside the app and run locally on your device. No data is sent to Google or to anyone else.

There are no advertising SDKs and no trackers. The app does not use the Android Advertising ID.

Data retention and deletion

Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Files you create with the app are stored on your device and remain entirely under your control; remove them as you would any other file.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.
